A compatibility and high-resolution patch for Seal of Evil.

Changes compared with the original game:
[list]
[*]Fixes Windows 11 startup compatibility issues without requiring Windows compatibility mode.
[*]Adds 1080p (1920x1080), 2K / 1440p (2560x1440) and 4K (3840x2160) options to the in-game settings, with borderless fullscreen.
[*]Adds enhanced opening screens and a widescreen campfire main-menu background, supplied as 4K artwork.
[*]Fixes corrupted skill-tooltip backgrounds and the associated multi-second hover freezes.
[*]Keeps overwrite-save confirmations over the fullscreen game instead of sending it to the desktop.
[/list]
