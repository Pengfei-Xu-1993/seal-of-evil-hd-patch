TOOLTIP RUNTIME EVIDENCE

The reported lower-HUD skill-tooltip fault was reproduced before the fix. At
1440p, the source was 2560x1440 while the background scratch bitmap was only
1024x768. Bottom tooltips exceeded its bounds and first/repeated hovers caused
approximately 4.7 seconds without a submitted frame while the pointer remained
over the icon. After resizing the scratch canvas, the same responses were
38.89 / 39.48 ms, with clean colors and the original translucent background.

Separate instrumented runs checked skill-panel and lower-HUD tooltips:

All captured events completed with zero dropped events or unfinished scopes.
Source and scratch dimensions matched; all observed tooltip rectangles stayed
in bounds. Native transparency remained enabled and format selectors remained
16. All 1207 / 833 / 355 observed shade-and-blit pairs completed normally at
1080p / 1440p / 4K. Screenshots confirmed clean backgrounds and readable text.

These responses measure WM_MOUSEMOVE to the next D3D8 Present entry after the
target tooltip draw, using CPU timestamps. They are not physical display or
input-to-photon latency measurements. The production DLL was separately smoke
tested at 1440p; first/repeat lower-HUD screenshots were clean and its runtime
log showed no diagnostic hooks. Its hash matches the packaged DLL above.

The change fixes the separate multi-second tooltip fault. It does not promise
smooth 4K frame rates: on the development machine, 4K world submission intervals
remained about 100 ms both away from and over the tooltip. The physical display
was 2560x1600; 3840x2160 was the actual render size scaled into its viewport.
Coverage is this controlled scenario, not every map or hardware configuration.
